# Liang-and-Markus for AI and Data topics

This project is for recording the communication information between Liang and Markus.

## During our meeting on October 16, focused on Agentic AI.
Liang highlighted four key challenges regarding the industrial deployment of AI agents:
Challenge 1: Context management. When a new session begins, a significant number of tokens are required to understand the progress of tasks from the previous session. Liang shared an open-source project addressing this: https://github.com/originoneai/awr
Challenge 2: Billing and cost management. This involves setting spending thresholds for individual agents and tracking their token consumption in real time.
Challenge 3: Defining the boundaries of human-agent collaboration—specifically, ensuring human intervention at critical junctures during an agent's execution process.
Challenge 4: Collaboration between multiple agents.

Markus discussed the future of Agentic AI, focusing on how different agents can work together—an intriguing area, especially regarding how they might coordinate while leveraging resources from various AI infrastructure (AI INFRA) platforms.
He also shared a project: https://paperclip.ing/
